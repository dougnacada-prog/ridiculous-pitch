A program that writes one line to /output/entry.txt — a single-sentence pitch for a bizarre startup idea that sounds ridiculous at first glance but makes sense as a real business — for make.fun's Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second bounty. The line: "We train ravens to deliver divorce papers: divorce lawyers pay $90 a bird because spouses who dodge process servers can't ignore a raven at the window — and the footage is the proof of service." (193 characters, one line.) A judge model compares entries two at a time and keeps the pitch that is both strangest and soundest, round after round, until one pitch stands.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
